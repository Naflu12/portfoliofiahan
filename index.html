About Hanira

A Story Told in Henna

Hanira Mehendi was born from a deep love for the ancient art of henna — a tradition where every swirl, dot and petal carries meaning. For nearly a decade, we have transformed the hands of brides, mothers and dreamers into living canvases of intricate artistry.

Each design is hand-drawn with precision, using only premium natural henna paste and essential oils. From the bold geometry of Arabic patterns to the lace-fine detail of traditional bridal work, every piece is composed to flatter the hand and tell a story.

  • ✦ 100% natural henna, hand-mixed fresh
  • ✦ Custom bridal consultations
  • ✦ Hygienic, single-use cones
  • ✦ On-location wedding services

Why Choose Us

The Hanira Difference

Four pillars that define our craft and care.

Premium Natural Henna

Hand-mixed paste using only organic henna powder and pure essential oils — never any chemicals or PPD. A deep, lasting stain guaranteed.

Bespoke Designs

Every design is composed freehand, uniquely for you. We blend motifs that hold personal meaning into patterns that flatter your hands.

Hygiene First

Single-use cones, sanitised tools and a spotless studio. Your safety and comfort are as important as the art itself.

On-Location Service

For weddings and large events, our team travels to your venue — bringing the studio experience to your celebration.
